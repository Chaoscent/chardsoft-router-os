Data limit

The router counts the mobile data it sends and receives. With your plan's data entered, it warns you in the Logs and on the dashboard before it runs out. The carrier's own count can differ a little; correct it below to match their app.

"Stop internet for devices" keeps the router and this dashboard reachable. You can resume from the dashboard at any time; the next period starts unblocked.

--
GNSS (satellite position)

The modem has a receiver for GPS, GLONASS, Galileo and BeiDou. It needs an antenna on the modem's GNSS port and a view of the sky. The position stays on the router; nothing is downloaded to speed it up, so the first fix can take a few minutes.

Without an RTC battery the Pi starts with a wrong clock until it reaches the internet; offline times and log times are wrong until then. With a position, GNSS sets it right away.

The track stays on the router and is drawn on the map. Switching it off deletes it.

Detailed map

--

Downloads that country's OpenStreetMap data once, from Protomaps' daily build, onto the router (a few hundred MB to several GB; over mobile data it counts towards your data limit). After that the map needs no internet and tells no one where the router is.
