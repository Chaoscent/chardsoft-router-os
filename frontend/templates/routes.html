Routes

Your routes

Send traffic for a network to a gateway of your choice, e.g. another router on your network that leads to 10.20.0.0/16. The router uses them, and so do your devices, as far as the firewall lets their traffic through. The internet connection itself is chosen on the Network page.

Add route

A gateway is enough in most cases: the router finds the interface by itself. Without a gateway, the destination must be reachable directly on the interface (e.g. a VPN tunnel). Changes wait for your confirmation, so a wrong route cannot lock you out.

How the router picks a table (rules)

Rules are checked from the lowest number up; the first table with a matching route wins. "Your devices" is the table for traffic from your network: it always leaves through the internet connection, even when another interface has a default route.
