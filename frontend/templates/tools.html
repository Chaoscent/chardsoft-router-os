Speed test from a device

Measures the Wi-Fi or cable between a phone or computer and the router, without the internet. Start the server, then run iperf3 on the device (an iperf3 app on a phone). The router waits five minutes for one test; port 5201 is open on your networks only while it waits.

Packet capture

Records the traffic on an interface into a file you open in Wireshark. It stops by itself after the time or at the size limit. Captures can contain other people's traffic and unencrypted passwords: starting one needs your password, and the file is deleted an hour after the capture ends.
